Open it on your phone

Point your camera at the code. It downloads the same APK directly, so you can skip typing the address.

Installing a sideloaded build

  1. Download the APK on the phone.
  2. Open it. Android asks once to allow installs from this browser; choose Allow from this source.
  3. Confirm Install, then open ShowMeOutfit and complete the short first-run setup.

This is an early alpha. The morning alert, permissions and weather source may change between builds. Everything stays on the device: no account, no backend, no analytics.
